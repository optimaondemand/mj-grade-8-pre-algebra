📚 VOCABULARY

Coinciding lines: Two lines that lie exactly on top of each other, sharing all points.

Consistent system: A system of equations that has at least one solution.

Dependent system: A system of equations in which the equations represent the same line, resulting in infinitely many solutions.

Independent system: A system of equations with exactly one solution, where the lines intersect at a single point.

Inconsistent system: A system of equations with no solution because the lines are parallel and never intersect.

Intersection: The point where two lines cross, representing the solution to a consistent and independent system.

Infinitely many solutions: Occurs when the equations in a system represent the same line, making every point on the line a solution.

No solution: Occurs when the lines in a system are parallel and never intersect.

One solution: Occurs when the lines in a system intersect at exactly one point.

Parallel lines: Lines that have the same slope but different y-intercepts, and therefore never intersect.

Solution to a system: The set of values for the variables that make all equations in the system true, represented by the point(s) of intersection.

Slope-intercept form: The equation of a line written as \(y=mx+b\), where \(m\) is the slope and \(b\) is the y-intercept.

System of equations: A set of two or more equations that are analyzed together to find a common solution.

💡 EXPLORING THE MATH

In today's lesson, we will explore what it means for a system of linear equations to have a solution when analyzed graphically. You will learn how to identify the solution to a system by examining where the lines intersect on a graph and what this intersection represents. Additionally, we will classify systems based on the number of solutions they have, using terms such as consistent, inconsistent, dependent, and independent. By the end of this lesson, you will be able to determine the relationship between the lines in a graph and confidently describe the solution to the system using appropriate terminology.


What is the Solution to a System Graphically?

A system of linear equations consists of two or more equations that you solve together to find a common solution. When the equations are written in \(y=mx+b\) form, they can be graphed as straight lines. The solution to a system of equations is the set of values for and  that make all the equations in the system true.

Graphically, the solution is represented by the point where the lines intersect. This point is called the point of intersection, and its coordinates \((x,y)\) satisfy both equations.


Number of Solutions and Classifications

The number of solutions a system of linear equations has depends on the relationship between the lines on the graph:

One Solution No Solution Infinitely Many Solutions
  • When the lines intersect they have exactly one solution.
  • The system is called consistent and independent.
  • The lines have different slopes.
  • When the lines are parallel they never intersect and have no solution.
  • The system is called inconsistent.
  • The lines have the same slope, but different y-intercepts.
  • When the lines coincide (lie exactly on top of each other, they have infinitely many solutions.
  • The system is called consistent and dependent.
  • The lines have the same slope and the same y-intercept.



Steps for Analyzing a Graph

  1. Identify the slopes (m) and y-intercepts (b) of the lines from the given equations.
  2. Examine the graph:
    • Do the lines intersect? If yes, how many times?
    • Are the lines parallel? Do they coincide?
  3. Classify the system based on the relationship between the lines:
    • Consistent and Independent: One solution.
    • Inconsistent: No solution.
    • Consistent and Dependent: Infinitely many solutions.


Example 1

Determine if the system is inconsistent, consistent dependent, or consistent independent.

Solution:
The lines intersect.

Therefore, the system has exactly one solution and is consistent independent.



Example 2

Determine if the system is inconsistent, consistent dependent, or consistent independent.

Solution:
The lines run parallel and do not intersect.

Therefore, the system has no solution and is inconsistent.


Conclusion

By analyzing the graph of a system of linear equations, you can determine the solution based on the relationship between the lines. Whether the system is consistent or inconsistent, dependent or independent, depends on whether the lines intersect, are parallel, or coincide. Understanding these classifications will help you confidently describe and analyze systems of equations in future problems. Practice identifying solutions graphically and classifying systems to build your skills!
