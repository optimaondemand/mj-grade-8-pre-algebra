📖 PRIOR KNOWLEDGE

In a previous lesson, you learned about the sum of the interior angles in a triangle and what the Triangle Sum theorem is. The Triangle Sum theorem is an important theorem for all polygons. As we get ready to explore the sum of angles in any polygon, it is important to review the sum of the angles in a triangle. All polygons can be divided into triangles. Triangles are the bases of all other polygons.


What is the Triangle Sum Theorem?

The Triangle Sum Theorem states that in any triangle, the total degrees of all three interior angles add up to exactly 180 degrees. Think of it as a fixed budget of degrees that must be distributed among the three angles. Understanding this theorem allows us to find unknown angles in triangles when we know the other angles.


If we have a triangle with angle measures of we can show that the sum of these angles will equal 180 degrees.


Conclusion

Today, we've delved into the realm of triangles, examining how their interior angles always add up to 180 degrees as per the Triangle Sum Theorem. This fundamental principle of geometry not only enhances our understanding of triangles but also aids in solving problems where we need to find missing angles. Whether working with exact measurements or algebraic expressions, knowing how to apply this theorem can open up a world of possibilities in understanding and applying geometry in real-world situations. Keep practicing these concepts, and soon you'll master the skill of finding any angle in a triangle!
