What is the Triangle Sum Theorem?

The Triangle Sum Theorem states that in any triangle, the total degrees of all three interior angles add up to exactly 180 degrees. Think of it as a fixed budget of degrees that must be distributed among the three angles. Understanding this theorem allows us to find unknown angles in triangles when we know the other angles.
