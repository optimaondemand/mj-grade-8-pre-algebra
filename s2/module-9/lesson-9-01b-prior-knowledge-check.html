Conclusion

Theoretical probability gives us a way to understand the chances of different events happening before they occur. By dividing the number of ways an event can happen by the total number of possible outcomes, we can find the probability of that event. This concept is a powerful tool in mathematics, allowing us to make informed predictions and decisions based on the likelihood of various outcomes. Remember, the key to mastering theoretical probability is practice, so keep working through different scenarios to sharpen your skills!
