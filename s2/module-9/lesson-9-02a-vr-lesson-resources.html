🎮 ACTIVITY YOU'LL COMPLETE

Create a simple experiment with one event, with no repetitions like rolling a die or drawing a marble from a bag. Create a few different examples of possible outcomes. Find and write the theoretical probabilities for the outcomes created. Then create a simulation of running the experiment with a certain number of times completed. Make a prediction of how many times the outcome might happen in the simulated experiment.

Next, create a repeated experiement or an experiment with 2 different events. Create a few different examples of possible outcomes. Find and write the theoretical probabilities for those outcomes created. Then create a simulation of running the experiment a certain number of times completed. Make predications of the outcomes based on how many times the experiment is completed.

Make a myrec explaining what the theoretical probability is and how to use it to make a prediction. Explain the prediction for the outcome in the simulated experiment. Does this mean that if the experiment is simulated this is exactly how many times this event will happen? Why or why not? What do chance and randomness have to do with how many times an outcome will actually happen? What does the prediction tell us and how is it useful?
