📖 PRIOR KNOWLEDGE

In a previous grade, you learned about basic geometric shapes and their properties. Among these, triangles stood out as a fundamental shape used across various mathematical applications. Today, we will revisit one specific type of triangle—the right triangle—and deepen our understanding of its unique features, such as the hypotenuse, the legs, and the right angle.


A right triangle is a type of triangle that has one angle measuring exactly 90 degrees, known as the right angle. This characteristic sets it apart from other triangles and introduces some special properties and formulas, like the Pythagorean Theorem, which are crucial for solving many mathematical problems.

1. The Right Angle The right angle is the angle in the triangle that measures 90 degrees. It is the defining feature of a right triangle. You can usually recognize it by a small square placed in the angle's corner in diagrams. This angle is significant because it directly influences the properties and calculations related to the triangle.

  • In the image above is the right angle.


2. The Legs The sides that form the right angle are known as the legs of the triangle. These two sides are often referred to simply as the "legs" or the "shorter sides" of the right triangle. They meet at the right angle and are perpendicular to each other. In calculations involving right triangles, such as finding area, these sides are often used to form the base and height.

  • In the image above are the legs of the right triangle.


3. The Hypotenuse The hypotenuse is the longest side of a right triangle and it lies opposite the right angle. This side is critical because, according to the Pythagorean Theorem, the square of the hypotenuse's length is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides’ lengths. In diagrams, the hypotenuse is always shown as the side facing the right angle's vertex and stretching across to the triangle's furthest point.

  • In the image above is the hypotenuse of the right triangle.


Understanding the position and role of each part of the right triangle is crucial. The hypotenuse acts as the base for many trigonometric applications and is often the key to solving many problems involving right triangles.


Conclusion

Today's review reinforced your knowledge of right triangles, focusing on their distinct parts: the right angle, the legs, and the hypotenuse. Each component plays a critical role in defining the properties and uses of right triangles in geometry. Remember, the legs form the right angle, and the hypotenuse is the longest side, opposite this angle. As you continue to study geometry, keep in mind how these elements affect both the theoretical aspects and practical applications of right triangles. This foundational understanding will be invaluable as you tackle more complex mathematical concepts in the future.
